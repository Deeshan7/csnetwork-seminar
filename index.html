Logical Types

Networks vary in size, complexity, and geographical spread.

  • Client-Server: a central powerful server, with multiple clients seeking resources. Used for large networks.
  • Peer-to-Peer: all nodes are equal, each both client and server. No dedicated servers. Ideal for small networks.

PAN

Personal Area Network

  • Belongs to a single person
  • Proximity up to 10 metres
  • Wired: USB. Wireless: Bluetooth, Infrared
  • Connects PCs, laptops, mobiles to transfer files
  • Examples: wireless keyboard/mouse, Bluetooth headphones, wireless printers, TV remotes
LAN

Local Area Network

  • Privately owned
  • Small geographical area: single room, floor, school, up to 1 km
  • Wired: Ethernet cables, UTP, coaxial, fibre optics. Wireless: WLAN
  • Key purpose: share resources (data, programs, printer)
  • Speed: 10 Mbps to 1 Gbps
